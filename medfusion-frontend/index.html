ABOUT MEDFUSION AI

Not 8 Separate Tools. One Unified Multimodal Fusion Engine.

Healthcare information is traditionally fragmented across isolated documents — an X-ray here, a blood report there. MedFusion AI solves this with Multimodal Health Intelligence.

🎯

Single Individual Scope

Designed specifically for the individual patient. Upload your own health information, review findings, and download reports without the clutter of complex hospital management systems.

🧬

Cross-Report Correlation

Findings from medical images and laboratory blood panels are linked using clinically verified templates and rule engines — never inventing unsupported medical conclusions.

⚡

Dynamic Card Display

If you upload only a Chest X-ray and a CBC report, the system displays only those relevant cards. No empty cards or distractions for modules you didn't upload.

THE PATIENT JOURNEY

How MedFusion AI Works

Upload → Analyze → Understand → Fuse → Combined Summary → Save Report.

01

Upload Everything

Drop chest/bone X-rays, skin photos, or PDF/image blood reports in a unified upload workflow.

02

AI & OCR Analysis

Deep CNNs classify medical imaging; EasyOCR extracts machine-readable numerical lab parameters.

03

Rule Engine Eval

Blood metrics are compared to configured clinical reference ranges and tagged: Low, Normal, or Elevated.

04

Multimodal Fusion

The Fusion Engine cross-correlates multi-source abnormalities into one synthesized health status.

05

Export & Timeline

Review your health summary, download a formatted PDF, and track historical milestones on your timeline.

DEEP LEARNING VISION

3 Medical Imaging Modules

Transfer learning models trained on benchmark medical imaging datasets.

🫁
ResNet-18

Chest X-ray

Evaluates pulmonary radiographs for pneumonia indicators versus clear normal findings.

Dataset: ChestX-ray14
Target: Normal vs Pneumonia detection
Output: Probability score & parenchymal status
🦴
EfficientNet-B0

Bone X-ray

Screens musculoskeletal radiographs for fractures and structural bone cortical continuity.

Dataset: MURA (Musculoskeletal Radiographs)
Regions: Hand, wrist, elbow, forearm, shoulder
Output: Fractured vs Not Fractured screening
🔬
EfficientNet-B0

Skin / Lesion Image

Analyzes skin photographs and dermoscopy for supported lesion classification categories.

Dataset: HAM10000 / ISIC
Classes: Melanocytic nevi, benign vs risk screening
Output: Classification probabilities & advice

OCR & RULE ENGINE

5 Laboratory Report Panels

A specialized CNN is not required for every lab type. Instead, MedFusion AI uses an OCR → Type Detection → Parameter Extraction → Rule Engine architecture with clinical reference ranges.

🩸

CBC Panel

Complete Blood Count

Extracts Hemoglobin, WBC, RBC, Platelets, and Hematocrit. Flags anemia or infection.

🍬

Diabetes Panel

Glycemic Profile

Evaluates Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), Postprandial Blood Sugar (PPBS), and HbA1c values.

🫀

Lipid Profile

Cardiovascular Risk

Parses Total Cholesterol, HDL (good), LDL (bad), and Triglycerides against thresholds.

💧

Kidney (KFT)

Renal Function

Checks Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea, and eGFR to evaluate kidney filtration health.

🧪

Liver (LFT)

Hepatic Function

Extracts ALT (SGPT), AST (SGOT), Total Bilirubin, and Albumin for hepatic status.
